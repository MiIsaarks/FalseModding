# Forgotten Skill Tweaks

A mod for Risk of Rain 2 that rebalances and slightly reworks the less used skills.

## Bandit Tweaks
* **Lights Out:** Kept it's original function, now also recharges the skills when hitting critical hits, but does not reset it's own cooldown. Upon hitting an enemy hemorrhaging guarantees critical strikes for 2 seconds, lights out also deals 2.5x more damage against hemorrhaging enemies.

* **Blast:** Increased damage to 380% and reduced bullet spread.

* **Serrated Dagger:** Can now hold two charges.

## Railgunner Tweaks
* **HH44:** Increases damage from 400% to 500%. Now consecutive weak spot hits add 100% damage to your next shot up to 10 consecutive hits for a 1000% extra damage, this also increase the damage of railgunner's special abilities, missing the weak spot resets the bonus damage to 0.

* **Polar Mines:** Decreased the cooldown from 12 seconds to 8, now can hold two charges. 

##  Acrid Tweaks
* **Blight:** Increases blight damage to 100% per second, adding a new blight stack increases the duration of every other stack by 2 seconds, increased the duration of each stack to 6 seconds. Blight can now crit and proc items, having a 0.65 proc Coefficient

##  False Son Tweaks
* **Laser of The Father:** Laser base charge duration reduced to 1s, Laser fire frequency now correctly scales with attack speed, proc coefficient per tick increased to 0.7, removed damage fall off, Growth now increases the damage by 5% instead of increasing the duration, increased the laser damage by 35%, refills 50% of the spikes.

* **Brother's Lightning:** Every 10 hits with the laser summons a lightning strike dealing 300% of the laser's damage to the target.

##  Huntress Tweaks
* **Arrow Rain:** Increased area. Cooldown reduced to 11s. Increased damage per second to 660%, increased proc coefficient to 0.7. increased tick frequency and now scales with attack speed.

* **Blink:** Cooldown reduced to 4.5s.

##  Artificer Tweaks
* **Flamethrower:** Greatly increased range. Adds a small jump on cast, tick damage increased by 2.5x, now has 100% ignite chance.



##  Installation
1. Install [BepInExPack](https://thunderstore.io/package/bbepis/BepInExPack/) and [R2API-Language](https://thunderstore.io/package/RiskofThunder/R2API_Language/).
2. Extract `ForgottenSkillsTweaks.dll` into your `BepInEx/plugins/` folder (or install directly via r2modman / Thunderstore Mod Manager).
